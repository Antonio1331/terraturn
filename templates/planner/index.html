NASA Earth Observation Harmonization & Ground-Truth Protocol

Multi-mission satellite telemetry is resampled onto a standardized 30-meter WGS84 analysis grid to reconcile spatial resolution differences before evaluating crop water budgets and erosion vulnerability.

STAGE 01 | INGESTION NASA Earthdata & POWER APIs

Automated retrieval via CMR, AppEEARS, and NASA POWER REST endpoints clipped to field boundaries.

STAGE 02 | DOWNSCALING 30m Covariate Resampling

9 km SMAP and 10 km GPM grids are downscaled using 30m SRTM topographic wetness and Landsat albedo.

STAGE 03 | ANOMALY FLAGS Bare-Soil & Thermal Stress

Time-series thresholding detects windows where NDVI < 0.25 overlaps with GPM storms or ECOSTRESS heat.

STAGE 04 | RANKING Constraint-Filtered Scoring

Rotations are filtered by soil texture, irrigation regime, and equipment, then weighted by user priorities.

Orbital Capabilities (Remote Sensing)

Wall-to-Wall Sub-Field Coverage: Resolves moisture and thermal gradients across headgates, ridges, and tailwater slopes.

Multi-Decadal Climate Baselines: Standardized NASA archives reveal long-term shifts in frost dates and heatwaves.

Objective Bare-Soil Tracking: Quantifies exact post-harvest ground exposure windows vulnerable to wind and storm runoff.

In-Situ Calibration (Human-in-the-Loop)

Subsurface Chemistry & Salinity: L-band radar senses top 5 cm moisture; root-zone pH, ECe salinity, and NPK require soil cores.

Biological & Pest Pressure: Nematodes and soil-borne pathogens require local scouting logs prior to legume selection.

Operational Timing: Cover-crop termination dates must be verified against field trafficability and local market contracts.
